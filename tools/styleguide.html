Style guide
BUS TRACKER · DRAWN FROM INDEX.HTML'S OWN STYLESHEET

Everything on this page is drawn with the tracker's own stylesheet and icons, read from index.html as this page loads, so it shows what ships. The tables are computed from that stylesheet too, including which rules use each token. tests/test-tokens.js fails when index.html or admin.html uses a size, corner, shadow or speed that is not one of these. The guide, the flyer and the briefing copy the same values; test-boot.js keeps the copies in step.

Colour

Four colours come in pairs, because they do two jobs. The first of each pair is a fill, a shape with white or near-black text on it, and it keeps its value in the dark theme. The second is the same colour used as text, and it inverts in dark, because maroon spelling a word on a near-black card is unreadable. Pick by what the colour is doing.

One family in two widths, served from assets/fonts under the SIL Open Font License. The condensed cut is for everything that is a label, a button, a name or a number; the normal width is for sentences. Seven sizes, nothing between them.

The seven sizes

Corners

Small parts, components, and the cards that hold them. A circle is 50% and a pill is 999px; there is no fourth corner.

Heights

A card resting on the page, a thing floating over it (a bus on the map, a popup, a toast), and an overlay. In dark the resting card has no shadow at all, because there is nothing to cast one onto; it is lifted by its own surface colour instead.

Motion

Three speeds and three curves. Things that arrive ease out; things that celebrate overshoot a little; vehicles accelerate and brake. Anything longer than a beat (a beep, a breathing badge, the parol's sway) states its own length, because it is a moment rather than a transition. With reduced motion asked for, the app flattens all of it; these lanes play only when pressed.

Icons

One family, drawn for this app: a 24-point grid, a 2-point stroke, round ends, the same corner as the coach. Each is a <symbol> in the sprite at the top of index.html's body, used as <svg class="gi"><use href="#i-…"/></svg>. Named for what they show and never for a social widget, because content blockers hide those by name: thank-you is i-ty, a sampaguita.
