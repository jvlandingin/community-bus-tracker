How your data is handled

Privacy policy for Bus Tracker, this website, and for its Android app on Google Play, Fibi: Bus Tracker PH.

If you're just watching the map

The app never asks for your location on its own, and you can use the whole map without ever giving it. No account, no name, no phone number. We can't see who is watching.

Two things can use your location if you ask them to, and neither one sends it anywhere: the location button on the map, which draws a dot where you are, and “Use my current location” in the stop picker, which finds the nearest stop to you. Both work entirely inside your phone. Your location is never transmitted, never stored on our side, and no other visitor can see it. The dot switches itself off when you close the page or the app.

If you save a stop, the stop is kept on your device and the distance to the bus is worked out there too — the page already knows where the buses are, so nothing extra is asked of the server and nothing about you is sent to it. If you tell a bus's popup “I'm on this bus”, the card follows that bus to your stop instead; which bus you picked is kept only until you close the page or the app, and is never sent either.

One thing is counted automatically, and it is worth saying plainly: while this page is open it sends a random ID every minute, so the organizer can see how many devices have the map open right now — a single number, nothing more. The ID is made up fresh by this tab, it isn't linked to you, your phone or anything else you do here, and it is deleted three minutes after you close the page. There is no way to go from that number back to a person, and no record of it is kept afterwards.

One more thing can leave your device, and only if you tap it: Salamat, on a bus, to thank whoever is carrying the phone. What's stored is a scrambled value that can't be traced back to you, can't be matched against anything else here, and exists only so a second tap on the same bus isn't counted twice — not your ID, not your location, not which bus you're on. It is deleted the moment that trip ends. Only the person sharing that bus can see the number. On our side it never adds up across trips: no running total is kept for any driver or any bus on the server. The sharer's own phone adds theirs up for their bus in My tickets, and that never leaves it.

And one more, also only if you tap it: Not a real bus?, on a bus, or Wrong?, on a sighting, to tell the organizer something on the map looks wrong. It is stored the same scrambled way as Salamat, so it can't be traced back to you and a second tap isn't counted twice. Only the organizer sees how many, not the sharer and not other riders, and it is deleted when that trip ends or the sighting expires. Nothing happens on its own: the organizer looks and decides.

“Send a link to this bus”, in a bus's popup, opens your phone's own sending sheet (or copies the link, where there isn't one) so you can pass it to someone. All the link carries is the bus's public ID, which every visitor's map already has, and it stops working when that trip ends. Nothing about you is in it, and the app sends it nowhere; you do.

If you share from the bus

What's sent: the bus's position, its direction, the bus number if you typed one, and a random ID made fresh for each trip, plus, only if you switched on “Show customized bus to riders”, a short code for how your bus is painted and dressed. Nothing about who you are. Each update overwrites the previous one, so no history of the trip exists anywhere. Not even we can see where a bus has been, only where it is now. When you tap Stop (or close the page), the bus disappears from everyone's map. Outside the route area, the bus shows on your own phone only, never on anyone else's map.

In the Android app, sharing keeps going with the screen locked or another app open, and a notification stays up for as long as it does, so it can never run without you knowing. The app sends exactly what this page sends and keeps no more: the latest position, in memory, until you stop. Swiping the app away stops sharing. Its GPS comes through Google Play services, as in most Android apps. If you get it from Google Play, Google knows your account installed it, as with any app there. Play shows us only totals, never who.

If riders tap Salamat on your bus, you'll see how many did — for this trip only, and only you. It isn't shown to other riders, it isn't shown to the bus company, and on our side it goes when the trip does. Your phone adds it to your bus in My tickets, a total only you can see.

Riders can also tell the organizer your bus doesn't look like a real one. Only the organizer sees that, for this trip only, and it goes when the trip does.

When you tap Stop, or the trip ends any other way, you get a ticket for the trip: where you got on and off, how long your bus was on the map, and how many riders said salamat. Your phone makes it from what it saw on the way, and it is not sent anywhere. Close it and it is gone.

Unless you tap Keep: then a copy goes into My tickets, on this phone only. The copy keeps the day, the direction, where you got on and off, the stamps, and a flower if anyone said salamat. It leaves out the times, how long the trip took, the bus number and how many riders thanked you, so a pile of kept tickets cannot time a trip or rank anyone. Nothing is ever kept unless you tap Keep on that ticket, and “Start from scratch” in My tickets deletes every copy.

Sightings

Picked from lists, never typed. Posted with no name attached and deleted automatically after a couple of hours. Anyone can tap Wrong? on one to tell the organizer, who can delete it.

To be fully honest

• The map's background images load from CARTO, a map provider. Like any website you visit, they can see your device's IP address. Everything else (code, fonts) is served from this site itself.
• The database runs on Supabase, which keeps standard server logs like any hosting service.
• Anyone who opens this site can see live bus positions, no key needed. That's the point of the tool, but worth saying plainly: while you share, anyone can watch, and could write down what they see as it happens. This site itself keeps only where the bus is now.
• Sharing normally needs the key from the group chat. The organizer can open it to everyone, and while it is open anyone at all can put a bus on the map, so a bus here is somebody's report, not a fact.

Open source

This tool is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence, which means anyone can read it, run it for their own route, and must keep their version open too. See the source code.

Who runs this

A community volunteer, not the bus company. This tool is not affiliated with, run by, or endorsed by Wonderful Transport. The route name and colours are used so you can tell which buses it tracks, nothing more. Positions come from passengers and crew who choose to share, so this is not an official schedule and the company is not responsible for it.

Accuracy is not guaranteed

Positions come from volunteers' phones, not company dispatch or sensors, so a bus can be a few minutes stale, briefly show the wrong direction before the app catches it, or be missing from the map when nobody happens to be sharing. This tool is provided as-is, with no warranty of accuracy or uptime, and is not liable for delays, missed connections, or any other loss from relying on it. If something looks wrong, trust the sightings board, the posted schedule, or your own eyes over the map.

Why you can trust this

This is a free community tool with no ads, nothing to sell, and no analytics beyond the single live count above — no profiles, no cookies, no third-party trackers. There is no business model that needs your data. There is simply nothing collected worth stealing. What is sent travels encrypted, over HTTPS.

Six small things are remembered on your own device, and nowhere else: whether this phone has been shown around yet, by the guide or the tour (so the dot on the guide button and the “New here?” note stop showing), your light or dark theme choice, the stop you saved, if you saved one, the tickets you chose to keep, if you kept any, your bus, if you have shared or finished a tour, and the trip you are sharing, while you share it. All six stay in your browser or the app, are never sent anywhere, and are not linked to anything. The Android app keeps a seventh: the key or PIN you entered to share, so you need not type it every trip. It goes to our server only to check it when you share, as it does in a browser, and it is deleted when it stops working.

Your bus is the coach your trips decorate in My tickets, and it is totals and choices, never a diary: how many trips you shared, how many of them were first on the map, end to end, before 5 AM or after dark, which directions, which places on the route, whether you finished each tour, your time on the map and the salamat you received in all, and how many weeks in a row you have shared, with the week of your last trip to count it from; and the spare parts it has (the app's own points, drawn as a nut: never bought with money, never sent), what they got, which paint and accessories it wears, whether you show it to riders, and, for the cap of three earning trips a day, the day of your last one. Totals, never a list: no clock times and no bus numbers, so it can say how much you have shared and the last day you did, but never a timetable of when you worked. The trip you are sharing is kept so that a page that reloads or closes mid-trip can carry on with the same bus, or still give you its ticket: when it started, roughly where it began and last was on the route, and the trip's random ID. It is deleted the moment the trip ends, or, if the page closed mid-trip, the next time the page opens.

Showing your bus to riders is off unless you switch it on, in My tickets or when the app first asks, once there is something on your bus to show. While it is on and you share, riders who tap your bus see it as you dressed it. The honest cost: they will be able to tell it is yours from one trip to the next. The code that carries it is letters from the app's own lists, never words or colours of anyone's choosing, and it is deleted with the trip, like the position.

A backup, from Your progress in My tickets, is a small file of your kept tickets and your bus, made on this phone and handed to you to keep wherever you like. The app never sends it anywhere. Restore reads one back.

Clearing your browser data, or uninstalling the app, removes them all, “Remove my stop” in the stop picker deletes the third on its own, and “Start from scratch” in My tickets the fourth and fifth.
