Test the app early

Fibi: Bus Tracker PH is this tracker as an Android app. It keeps sharing with your screen locked.

What I need from you

Google needs 12 testers to use it for 14 days in a row before it can go public. It checks that they really use it.

  • Use it when you ride. Tap Start on the bus, then lock your phone.
  • Check the map while you wait. See where your bus is.
  • Tell me what's wrong or confusing. A screenshot beats a paragraph.
  • Stay in the group until it's public. Leaving takes you off the count.

Join in 3 steps

On the phone you ride with, signed in to its Google account.

Already a tester? Open it on Google Play

  1. 1
    Join the tester group

    Tap Join group. Any name is fine, and pick No email.

    Says you don't have permission? That's normal. Tap Join group.

  2. 2
    Become a tester

    Tap the blue Become a tester button.

    Says App not available? Do step 1 first, then open this again.

  3. 3
    Install it from Google Play

    Then open it once. This works after step 2.

On an iPhone?

The test is Android only. Use the tracker in Safari: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
